# Great-Expectations
I don't have a specific dream job. However, I have several dream characteristics of a job I would want, such as:
1. A good work/life balance that allows me to leave any work stressors behind when I go home.
2. Something that pays a comfortable living wage.
3. A job where I can use my skills in technology and problem-solving, even if it is not necessarily a tech industry job.
4. A job that gives me opportunities to continue developing new skills.
5. A company that has a positive and friendly work environment.
